Privacy policy. Last updated 2 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. truSQRL does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it does not track you.

How the game works

  • The whole game runs on your device. The game makes no network requests.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.
  • The game does not ask for your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your points, what you have bought in the gun store and your settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple

Downloads are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not ours.
