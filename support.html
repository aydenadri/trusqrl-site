Controls

  • Left thumb: walk. Push to the edge to run.
  • Right thumb: drag anywhere to look.
  • FIRE: shoot. With the rifle the bolt cycles itself.
  • AIM: shoulder the rifle and look through the scope.
  • RANGE: the rangefinder, on the squirrel.
  • CROUCH: steadier and lower.
  • M18 or RIFLE: swap to the pistol and back.
  • SIGHT: the scope or the red dot.
  • CARD: the range card.
  • The two bars at the top right pause the game and open the menu.

The same list is in the game under HOW TO PLAY.

If it runs slowly

Open SETTINGS from the menu and lower QUALITY. Lower is smoother.

Saves

Your points, what you have bought and your settings live on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Playing offline

Everything is in the app. It plays the same in airplane mode.

Sound

If there is no sound, check the silent switch and turn the volume up, then tap the screen once.
